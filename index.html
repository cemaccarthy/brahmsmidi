About the composer

Johannes Brahms (1833–1897) was a German composer and pianist, and one of the towering figures of Romantic music, often named alongside Bach and Beethoven as one of music's "three Bs."

Fittingly for this app, Brahms also wrote the world's best-known lullaby: the 1868 "Wiegenlied," better known today simply as Brahms' Lullaby. Its slow, steady rhythm sits close to a resting heartbeat, which is part of why it has soothed listeners to sleep for over 150 years.
